## QuestFit - Türkçe

QuestFit, klasik kalori takibi ve spor planlamasını RPG (Rol Yapma Oyunu) dinamikleriyle birleştiren yenilikçi bir sağlık ve fitness uygulamasıdır.

### Temel Özellikler
* Kapsamli Profilleme: Yas, boy, kilo ve vücut tipine (Ektomorf, Mezomorf, Endomorf) gore kisisellestirilmis BMR ve TDEE (Gunluk Kalori Ihtiyaci) hesaplamalari.
* Akilli Beslenme Takibi: Barkod okuyucu ve yapay zeka destekli gorsel tanima (Groq Vision) ile saniyeler icinde ogunlerinizi kaydedin. Bilinmeyen besinler icin AI tarafindan otomatik makro (Protein, Karbonhidrat, Yag) hesaplamasi yapilir.
* Oyunlastirma ve RPG: Vucut tipinize gore degisen dinamik 2D avatarlar, kalori hedeflerini tutturdukca ve spor yaptikca kazanilan XP'ler, seviye atlama sistemi ve her gun yenilenen rastgele 30 farkli gorev.
* Yapay Zeka Destekli Antrenmanlar: Bulundugunuz ortama ve sahip oldugunuz ekipmanlara gore Groq AI kullanılarak aninda size ozel idman programlari olusturulur. Ayrica "Kas Yorgunlugu" sistemi ile asiri antrenman engellenir.
* Donanim Entegrasyonu: Akilli pedometre sistemi ile gercek zamanli adimsayar ve kalori takibi.

### Kurulum
1. Projeyi bilgisayariniza klonlayin.
2. Bagimliliklari yuklemek icin terminalde `npm install` komutunu calistirin.
3. Uygulamayi baslatmak icin `npx expo start` yazin.

## QuestFit - English

QuestFit is an innovative health and fitness application that combines classic calorie tracking and workout planning with RPG (Role Playing Game) dynamics.

### Core Features
* Comprehensive Profiling: Personalized BMR and TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) calculations based on age, height, weight, and body type (Ectomorph, Mesomorph, Endomorph).
* Smart Nutrition Tracking: Log your meals in seconds using a barcode scanner and AI-powered visual recognition (Groq Vision). For unknown foods, AI automatically calculates macros (Protein, Carbs, Fat).
* Gamification & RPG: Dynamic 2D avatars that change based on body type, XP earned by hitting calorie targets and working out, a leveling system, and 30 different randomized daily quests.
* AI-Powered Workouts: Instant, custom workout routines generated by Groq AI based on your environment and available equipment. Also includes a "Muscle Fatigue" system to prevent overtraining.
* Hardware Integration: Real-time pedometer and calorie tracking with smart sensor integration.

### Installation
1. Clone the repository to your local machine.
2. Run `npm install` in your terminal to install dependencies.
3. Start the application by running `npx expo start`.
